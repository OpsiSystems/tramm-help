Required Setup

These entities form the core of your Tramm environment. Every other feature — planning, execution, and costing — depends on them. Set them up before loading operational data.

Settings

System and user preferences for your Tramm environment.

View guide →

Trailers

Optional passive transport assets towed by horse vehicles. Set up before Vehicle Classes that reference them.

View guide →

Vehicle Classes

Templates that define capacity, dimensions, and costs for groups of vehicles.

Vehicles

Individual vehicles that inherit their properties from a Vehicle Class.

View guide →

Site Classes

Templates for operating hours and durations shared across similar sites.

View guide →

Sites

Depots, warehouses, and delivery points — the locations in your network.

Product Groups

Templates for shared product properties and attributes.

Products

The goods being transported, with sizes and loading rates.

Common Additions

Extend your setup as your operation grows. These entities are not required for basic planning, but most deployments add some or all of them — drivers, third-party carriers, geographic zones, and trackable transport equipment.

Clients

Companies whose orders are planned and delivered.

Transporters

Third-party carriers used for outsourced trips.

Fleets

Named groups of vehicles for operational or reporting purposes.

Operators

Drivers and other personnel assigned to trips.

Regions & Lanes

Transport Units

Specialist Configuration

Advanced features and app-specific configuration. Some of these require specific Tramm extensions (such as Transact) — check with your implementation team which apply to your environment.

Routing Classes

Controls routing parameters for the Planner — vehicle dimensions, speed limits, road-type preferences, and no-go zones. Required only for specialist vehicle types; standard planning works without one.

View guide →

Entity Types

Custom type labels and attribute rule sets applied across vehicles, sites, and other entities.

View guide →

Transport Segments

Predefined legs in a multi-modal or crossdock network. Planner-specific.

View guide →

Master Routes

Saved route templates for recurring trips, configured in the separate Master Route Builder app.

View guide →

Route Sets

Named collections of master routes, used for grouping and optional automatic trip generation.

View guide →

Master Charges

Surcharge rules calculated automatically on top of base trip costs. Requires the Transact extension.
