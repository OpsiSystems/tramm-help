Trip Lifecycle

Every trip moves through a numbered status sequence as it progresses from planning through to completion. FleetVision receives this status from Tramm and displays it on the trip record. The status numbers are internal — users see the descriptions.

0 Tracking Vehicle is being tracked by GPS but has no trip assigned yet.
30 Scheduled Trip is planned and scheduled in Tramm. Vehicle has not yet departed.
40 Confirmed Trip has been confirmed — operational acknowledgement that it will run.
50 Fleet Confirmed The fleet team has confirmed the trip and vehicle assignment.
60 Vehicle Confirmed The specific vehicle has been confirmed for this trip.
65 Loaded Vehicle has been loaded with the consignment and is ready to depart.
70 Departed (GPS) GPS has detected that the vehicle has left the departure point and is on its way. Live
80 Dispatched Trip has been formally dispatched by the operations team. Live
90 Returning (GPS) GPS has detected the vehicle is on its way back to base after completing deliveries. Live
100 Completed (GPS) GPS has confirmed the trip is complete — the vehicle has returned.
110 Delivered All deliveries have been recorded as complete in the system.
120 Debriefed Trip has been closed out — debrief complete, all discrepancies recorded.

Trip Display Status

The display status is what FleetVision shows on the map and dashboard — it's a calculated label that combines the trip lifecycle status with GPS data and schedule timing. It answers the question: how is this trip performing right now?

Note: The display status is separate from the trip lifecycle status above. A trip can be in Departed (GPS) lifecycle status but show Late as its display status if it has fallen behind schedule.
No Vehicle Allocated Trip has no vehicle assigned. Cannot be tracked.
No GPS A vehicle is assigned but no GPS signal is being received.
Planned Start Trip has not yet started — waiting for the planned departure time.
Delayed Start The planned start time has passed but the vehicle has not yet departed.
Wrong Trip GPS activity has been detected, but it does not match the planned route for this trip.
On Time Trip is running on or ahead of schedule.
Late Trip has exceeded the late threshold set for this domain.
Very Late Trip has exceeded the very late threshold — escalation may be required.
Forced Complete Trip was manually closed by an operator before GPS confirmation was received.
Completed Trip is fully complete — GPS confirmed the vehicle has returned.

Stop Statuses

Each delivery stop on a trip has its own status, shown as a coloured circle on the map. The circle colour tells you at a glance whether each stop was completed, skipped, or something else went wrong.

S
Scheduled Stop is planned but the vehicle has not yet arrived. Upcoming on the route.
A
Arrived Vehicle is currently at this stop.
D
Departed Vehicle visited and left this stop. Delivery recorded.
K
Skipped Stop was not visited — the vehicle moved past without stopping. Requires follow-up.
Ab
Aborted Stop was cancelled — delivery was not attempted (e.g. site closed, refused delivery).
U
Unplanned Vehicle stopped here, but this stop was not in the original route. May indicate an unauthorised stop.

Vehicle Statuses

Vehicle icons on the map change colour based on what the GPS reports the vehicle is doing. The icon colour tells you at a glance whether the vehicle is moving, stationary, on a trip, or off-network.

🚛
Moving — On Trip
Vehicle is moving and has an active trip assigned. All good.
🚛
Stopped — At Planned Stop
Vehicle is stationary at a scheduled delivery stop on its route.
🚛
Stopped — At Unplanned Stop
Vehicle is stationary at a location that was not on its planned route. May require attention.
🚛
Moving — No Trip
GPS shows the vehicle is moving, but no trip is assigned. Check allocation.
🚛
Stopped — No Trip
Vehicle is stationary with no active trip. Parked at base or idle.

Delivery Statuses

Delivery statuses appear in FleetVision's analysis charts and summary reports. They reflect the outcome of each consignment (order/load) at the end of a trip.

Fully Done All items on the consignment were delivered successfully.
Partially Done Some items were delivered but the consignment was not fully completed.
Scheduled Delivery is planned for this trip but has not yet been attempted.
Not Done Delivery was not completed — no items from this consignment were delivered.
Aborted Delivery was actively cancelled — the stop was visited but delivery did not proceed.
Total Aggregate figure used in charts — represents all consignments combined.
